## Design

Own answer first, as in round 3. After the reveal, one tap: which of three lines is
closest to what you would say, or none. Then optionally how close, 0 to 3. Lineup passes
when the pick is the prediction. Targets: pick rate ≥ 70%, mean closeness ≥ 2.0.

Predictions follow the round 3 correction: mock-grandiosity with money and ambition as the
default object (#1, #2, #3, #6, #10, #11, #12), a flat number or condition as the dodge
(#5), no joke for a statement, logistics or a stranger's real issue (#4, #7, #9), and a
flat counter to "be serious" (#8).
